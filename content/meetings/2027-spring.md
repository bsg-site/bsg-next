The BSG will run sessions at the BCA Spring Meeting 2027. The meeting, *Diversity in Diffraction*, takes place in person at the University of York from Monday 22 to Wednesday 24 March 2027.

## Registration and abstracts

**[Registration is open](https://registrations.hg3conferences.co.uk/hg3/frontend/reg/thome.csp?pageID=156758&eventID=380)** on the conference website, which also has the venue and accommodation.

- **18 January 2027:** deadline for abstracts (submission opens shortly)
- **February 2027:** notification of abstract acceptance
- **26 February 2027, 23:59:** early bird registration closes
- **18 March 2027, 23:59:** registration closes

Questions about registration: [events@hg3.co.uk](mailto:events@hg3.co.uk).

## Programme

The [draft programme](https://registrations.hg3conferences.co.uk/hg3/frontend/reg/tOtherPage.csp?pageID=156527&ef_sel_menu=2876&eventID=380) is on the conference website. The BSG Plenary, by Andrew Lovering (University of Birmingham), is on Tuesday 23 March at 17:10. BSG sessions run in the parallel sessions on Tuesday and Wednesday; details will follow once the programme is confirmed.

Early-career researchers can also join the Early Stage Crystallographers Group satellite meeting on Monday 22 March, before the main meeting opens.
